The Drift Layer

The Drift Layer is a publication. It holds papers, and it holds the definitions used to measure whether software spending money on a person's behalf stayed inside the authority that person gave it. It is not an organisation, a service or a product, and nothing here is for sale.

The layer this is named for

Whether an agent acted inside its authority is decided, if it is decided at all, above the eventual movement of money — and the payment message visible downstream was never built to record that decision.

That plane is the drift layer: where authority is created, evaluated against a proposed transaction, and, when execution departs from it, either repaired or thrown away. A payment rail records that money moved. It does not record which authority permitted it, or what was checked before it did.

Drift, named precisely

Three different things travel under the word. This publication keeps them apart and does not use drift unqualified.

Behavioural drift — an agent departing from its onboarding profile.

Intent drift — constraints lost while an instruction is interpreted, before any mandate exists.

Mandate divergence — the executed transaction leaving the committed mandate.

Of the three, mandate divergence is the one measurable at the transaction, because the mandate and the committed transaction are both artefacts that can be held, compared, and shown to someone else. It is the sense used throughout this site.
